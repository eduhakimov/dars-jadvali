# Timetable & Deadline Reminder

## First setup
1. On the **Settings** page check the class periods (default 08:30–09:50, 10:00–11:20 ...) and click **Save**.
2. Enter the semester start date. The week containing it is week 1 (odd).
3. Tick your working days.

## Add classes
- On the **Week** page click an empty cell or **Add class** (`Ctrl+L`).
- Set the subject, type (lecture, practical, lab, seminar), group, room and weeks:
  *Every week*, *Odd weeks only* or *Even weeks only*.
- Double-click a class to edit it; right-click for duplicate and delete.
- Use the arrows to browse weeks. **Both weeks** shows odd and even classes together.

## Today
The current or next class, time until it starts, today's classes and tasks due in the next 3 days.

## Tasks
- Type a task in the top field and press `Enter`, or press `Ctrl+N` for the full form.
- Click the box on the left or press `Space` to mark a task done.
- Filters: Active, Overdue, Done, All.

## Notifications
While the app is open or in the tray, a Windows notification appears N minutes before a class and
1 day / 3 hours / 1 hour before a deadline. Closing the window keeps the app in the tray.
**Start with Windows** is only enabled if you tick it yourself.

## Export
The **Export** menu on the **Week** page saves the timetable as PDF or PNG, or prints it.
JSON export/import in the **File** menu is for backups and moving to another computer.
